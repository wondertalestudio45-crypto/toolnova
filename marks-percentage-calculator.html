Frequently asked questions

Which grading system is used?

A common Pakistani board scale: A1 80%+, A 70%+, B 60%+, C 50%+, D 40%+, E 33%+. Your board may differ.

How is percentage calculated?

Marks obtained ÷ total marks × 100.
