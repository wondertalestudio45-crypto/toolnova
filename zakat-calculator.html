Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab?

Commonly 52.5 tola (612.36 g) of silver or 7.5 tola (87.48 g) of gold. This tool uses the silver nisab.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It is a calculation aid. Please ask a qualified scholar about your situation.

Which prices should I use?

Use today's local market price per gram.
